# Loothour — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Loothour is a focus timer for Android. This policy describes what the app does with information.

## What the app stores
Loothour stores the following on your device only:
- the tasks you type before each focus block;
- your focus sessions (start, end and break times);
- your progress (XP, gold, level, items you bought, pets you hatched);
- your settings (theme, sound effects, music).

## What the app does not do
- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It does not send any information to the developer or to anyone else. The release version does not request the Internet permission.
- It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Your data
Everything stays on your phone. Inside the app, Settings > "Reset data" deletes all of it. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. Because the developer never receives your data, the developer cannot look it up or recover it.

## Children
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If a future version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released.
